## Interpretation

H1 supported for the tested common inputs; H2 supported for all tested slow/error/mixed inputs. Source snapshot shows one classification pass, generated bitmap rejection, protocol work bits and bulk CopyTo on the common path. Slow output reserves capacity once. Compiler diagnostics retain the projection and copy paths (global sinks prevent elimination). Profiles show baseline Map.Get/PutEmpty frames and candidate CopyTo frames. Both macOS CPU profiles are dominated by runtime.kevent and have sparse application samples; do not interpret their percentages as a precise cost breakdown. Timing and allocation effects are attributable only to the combined change, not to bitmap alone. Independent ownership prevents a zero-allocation claim.
